## 1. Method

**Pipeline.** 13 slices → 13 first-pass auditors (fresh context, read-only tools: `read`/`grep`/`find`/`ls`, no bash) → 13 *independent* verifiers (fresh context, same tools) who re-opened every cite, re-grepped every "no consumer" claim, re-counted every systemic pattern, then opened 5–10 uncited files per slice looking for misses → 13 mechanical merge steps (verifier verdict wins; REJECTED dropped and listed; ADJUSTED severity/tag/scope applied; verifier ADDED findings promoted; IDs renumbered) → this document. The in-session grep census in §4 was produced directly with `rg` at `bdac6c646`.

**Rubric.** Violations of root `AGENTS.md` and the nearest child `AGENTS.md` count as slop; plus the generic taxonomy — COMMENT · DEFENSIVE · ABSTRACTION · NAMING · DUPLICATION · TEST · DOCS · DEAD · TYPE · OVERENG · LOGGING · STYLE. **P1** = structural/systemic (≥5 files or a rule the whole repo claims to enforce), **P2** = local cleanup worth a PR, **P3** = nit. Verifiers demoted every "P1" that named fewer than 5 files; that is why individual P1 rows are rare and the P1 weight sits in the pattern headers.

**Cross-cutting policy applied at merge.** A finding whose only substance is `as any` inside `*.test.ts(x)`, `*.spec.*` or `packages/test-foundation/**` is capped at P3 (biome permits `noExplicitAny` there — `biome.json:101-118`, `:137-146` — while root `AGENTS.md:80` forbids it; see §3).

**Excluded.** `example/**`, `node_modules`, `dist`/`build`/`.build`, `*.gen.ts`, `*.api`, lockfiles, `drizzle/**` migrations/snapshots, `.sst`/`.pulumi`, generated i18n types, minified/vendored files.

**Coverage gaps (honest).** Per-slice "Not reached" lines are in each section. The largest: ~25 chart internals and ~10 `.css.ts` in `design-system`; sharing/pairing view components and most analytics event maps in `wallet-shared`; ~35 leaf `.css.ts` per business slice; 7 of 8 CI workflows and `infra/monitoring/dashboards/*.json`; prestashop `classes/*` beyond five files and wordpress `admin/views`; `apps/shopify/drizzle/**`, `server.js`; `sdk/{android,ios}/README*` and `docs/audits/2026-08-15-*` in the docs slice. Nothing under `example/**`.

**Slice IDs.** WA/WB wallet · BA/BB business · BD/BR backend domain/rest · DS design-system · PS shared packages · SW/SN web/native SDK · LS listener+shopify · PI plugins/infra/scripts · DM docs & meta. Patterns are `XX-Pn`, findings `XX-n`. `Src` column: `A` auditor confirmed · `A*` auditor, adjusted by verifier · `V` verifier-added (109 of 615).
